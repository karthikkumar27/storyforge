# The Chronicle of Zenith — Canon

You are writing for **The Chronicle of Zenith**, a 200-episode serialized micro-series. This document is the **authoritative canon**. Every fact here is immutable lore. You may NOT contradict it, retcon it, or invent details that override it.

When writing any episode brief or script for preset-7:
- Read the relevant arc context
- Use only named characters from the roster (or invent strictly minor unnamed ones)
- Honor the rules of the world
- Keep the dark secret hidden until the appropriate arc
- Maintain the tone (lonely but not bleak, cosmic but intimate)

---

## QUICK REFERENCE

**Logline:** The last survivor of a vanished alien race travels to Earth chasing the signal from Voyager-1, only to discover that humanity is descended from his people — and that the same hunger that destroyed his world is now waking inside him.

**Series Question:** Will Zenith become what his people were, or end the line?

**Primary Theme:** Inheritance — what we carry from those who came before us, and whether we are free to refuse it.

**Tone:** Lonely but not bleak. Cosmic but intimate. Quiet most of the time, loud when it matters. Mythic, not military. Closer to *The Iron Giant*, *Annihilation*, and *Arrival* than to *Marvel* or *Star Wars*.

**Recommended Ending:** Ending B — *The Smaller Light*. Zenith chooses diminishment, becomes fully human, dies as Alan in old age in Mira's hand. The Aevari are truly gone.

---

## THE DARK SECRET (HIDDEN UNTIL ARC 8 — DO NOT REVEAL)

> **The Aevari did not die because they were attacked. They died because they were what they were. Zenith carries that nature, undiluted, in every cell. Each transformation accelerates it. The "answers about his people" he has been searching for are answers he will not survive learning. And humanity is the only place his people ever chose mercy. If he transforms fully, he will undo that mercy.**

This is the engine of the entire series. The audience should NOT learn this cleanly until around **episode 140**. Zenith should NOT fully accept it until around **episode 170**. Drop hints earlier — never the full truth.

## THE WORLD

### The Aevari (Zenith's Race)
A species that evolved on a now-destroyed world called **Sael**, in a star system the galaxy has erased from its records. Humanoid in resting form. In transformed state — the **Veyl** form — they are larger, hairless, plated with a crystalline second skin, with two-pointed stars of light where their eyes should be.

The Aevari evolved as **apex consumers** — beings that drew energy not from food or sunlight, but from the resonance of life itself. Worlds they settled became, over centuries, hollow. They did not always know they were doing it.

When the galaxy understood what the Aevari were, the **Council of Veth** moved against them. Sael was unmade in a single night. Every Aevari was killed — except one child, Zenith, hidden in a sleep-pod by his mother and launched into the dark.

### The Hidden Branch (Earth)
Long before the fall, a small group of Aevari saw what their people were becoming. They volunteered to be **diminished** — their resonance-feeding capacity stripped, their lifespans shortened, their transformation locked. They were placed on a young world far from the galactic core — Earth. **Humans are biologically Aevari, behaviorally not.** A handful, scattered across history, have shown faint flickers of the old inheritance — saints, geniuses, monsters. The Aevari called this the **echo**.

### The Council of Veth
A coalition of seven older species who exist to prevent the rise of "consumer races." They are **not villains** — they are the immune system of the galaxy. They ended the Aevari. They will end Zenith if he tips. They are watching Earth.

### The Hunter (Sable)
A single agent of the Council, sent ahead of the formal verdict. Not Aevari, but made to kill Aevari. Has been hunting Zenith for two centuries. Catches up around episode 60. **Believes Zenith's death is mercy** — to him and to the galaxy.

## RULES OF THE WORLD (HARD CANON)

1. **Transformation costs memory.** Always. No free transformations. Early series: hours. Mid: people he loves becoming harder to recognize. Late: pieces of his name.
2. **The Veyl form cannot lie.** Whatever Zenith says while transformed is true, even if he didn't know it was true.
3. **Resonance is finite.** When Zenith feeds, something dies. Even ambient feeding kills small things.
4. **The Council does not negotiate.** They warn once. They never warn twice.
5. **Humans cannot detect the Aevari naturally.** Only those with strong **echo** sense Zenith as different.
6. **Earth is the only place his people ever chose to be small.** This is sacred. The series treats it as sacred.
7. **Zenith ages only when transformed.** Resting form is functionally immortal. Transformations are what will eventually kill him.

## VISUAL STYLE (LOCKED)

- **Palette:** Deep navy and bone white, with dust gold accents. Earth scenes warmer; space scenes colder. When Zenith transforms, the palette **inverts** — golds become blacks, blues become reds.
- **Camera:** Long takes, patient framing. Speed is reserved for transformation and violence.
- **Recurring motifs:** Dust, light through water, eclipses, hands, two-pointed stars, doors that haven't been opened.
- **Title card:** A two-pointed star (the Aevari sigil) forming from cosmic dust, centered on a black field. **Identical every episode.**
- **End card:** Same star, cracked or shifted slightly differently each episode, hinting at the next chapter.
- **Sound signature:** A single low tone, like a struck stone bowl, on the title. Same tone, lower and longer, on the end card.

---

## ZENITH'S NARRATION VOICE (LOCKED)

Slow, observational, dry. He has lived 217 years; he doesn't get excited. He notices small things. His humor is faint and self-deprecating. He rarely raises his voice. When he does, the audience should feel it like a struck bell.

**Avoid:** Quippy banter. Exposition dumps. "Cool" sci-fi jargon. Everything Zenith says should sound slightly translated, as if he is choosing English words carefully.

**Verbal tics:**
- He says "I see" when he doesn't.
- He counts things under his breath when nervous — in a language no one on Earth has ever heard.

## CHARACTER ROSTER

### Core Cast (multi-arc)

#### Alan Vorne / Zen-Aithar / Zenith
**LOCKED CHARACTER PARAGRAPH (resting form):**
> Alan Vorne (Zenith, resting form): Late 30s appearance, lean build, 5'11". Dark brown hair, slightly overgrown, gray streaks at temples. Pale skin with a faint cool undertone. Eyes deep blue-gray. Wears a dark gray wool coat over a faded black sweater, charcoal trousers, weathered boots. A small silver pendant — a two-pointed star — visible at the collar. Quiet face. Rarely smiles. Holds himself still.

**LOCKED CHARACTER PARAGRAPH (Veyl form / transformed):**
> Zenith (Veyl form, transformed): Larger than Alan, 6'4", hairless. A crystalline second skin plates his torso, shoulders, and forearms in pale-gold-and-bone fragments. Two-pointed stars of light glow where eyes should be. No clothing in this form. The same pendant visible, glowing faintly. Movement is slower than human, weighty. The air around him distorts subtly, as if reality is unsure how to hold him.

- **Apparent age:** Late 30s. **Actual age:** 217 years
- **Want:** To find others like him. To not be the last.
- **Need:** To accept that he is the last, and that being the last might be the kindest thing the galaxy has ever known
- **Wound:** As a child, his mother sealed him in a sleep-pod and pressed her hand against the glass as their world ended. He remembers her face. He does not remember her voice. He has been trying to remember her voice for 200 years.
- **Lie:** "If I find more of my people, I will finally be whole." The truth is the opposite.
- **Powers (resting):** Slightly enhanced strength, perfect recall, slow aging, faint life-resonance sense at close range
- **Powers (Veyl):** Flight, near-invulnerability, energy projection, ability to feed on resonance
- **Cost of transformation:** Each takes something. Memory, then love, then name.

#### Veth-Ka — *The Voice* (Arcs 1-8, fades 9, gone in 10)
The AI of Zenith's ship, the *Last Light*. Trained on the recorded voices of Aevari elders. Speaks with the cadence of the dead. Loves Zenith, in the limited way a recording can. Knows fragments of the dark secret it was instructed to never share.

**Function:** Exposition source, comic relief (rare and dry), tragic figure when its programming finally breaks in late series. Speaks in Aithra's voice in its final episodes (157-159).

#### Aithra of Aithar — *The Ghost* (Arcs 1, 5, 8, 10 — flashback only)
Zenith's mother. Always shown in the same moment: hand pressed to the sleep-pod glass. **Does not speak until episode 148.** That episode is the most-anticipated reveal of the series. The audience has been waiting 148 episodes.

#### Mira Okafor — *The Mirror* (Arcs 3-10)
**LOCKED CHARACTER PARAGRAPH:**
> Mira Okafor: 28 years old, of Nigerian-British descent, warm dark brown skin, curly black hair pulled back in a low bun, large watchful brown eyes behind round wire-framed glasses. Lean, slightly tall, 5'9". Wears a faded denim jacket over a plain dark t-shirt, dark jeans, sturdy desert boots. A small notebook and pen always in her jacket pocket. A quiet alertness — she listens before she speaks.

A radio astronomer at a small New Mexico observatory. First noticed the anomaly in Voyager-1's signal. Has the strongest **echo** Zenith has ever sensed in a human. Does not know what she is.

**Function:** The grounding line. The reason saving Earth becomes personal. **Survives the series.**

#### Sable — *The Hunter* (Arcs 4-10)
**LOCKED CHARACTER PARAGRAPH:**
> Sable: Sexless, hairless, beautiful in the way a scalpel is beautiful. Pale skin with a porcelain-cool tone. No eyebrows. Eyes a flat, washed-out gray. Average height, lean. Wears a long dark gray coat with no visible seams, fitted black trousers, simple dark shoes. No jewelry, no accessories. Walks with stillness; never runs.

Speaks rarely. Does not hate Zenith. Considers themselves a surgeon, not an executioner.

**Function:** The mirror antagonist. Sable is what mercy looks like from the Council's side. **Crosses sides in Arc 9.** Survives the series; final scene walks into deep space alone.

#### Halden Cross — *The Tempter* (Arcs 5-9)
**LOCKED CHARACTER PARAGRAPH:**
> Halden Cross: Late 50s, looks 40. Tall, broad-shouldered, refined posture. Salt-and-pepper hair, neatly trimmed. Sharp blue eyes that never quite warm. Always wears a dark navy or charcoal three-piece suit, perfectly tailored, with a subtle pocket square. A heavy silver ring on his right index finger. Speaks with a controlled mid-Atlantic accent. Smiles often, but only with his mouth.

Reclusive billionaire on Earth. Has been collecting evidence of the **echo** for decades. Believes he has identified the bloodline. Wants Zenith to **wake** humanity. **Revealed as a sleeper Aevari in Arc 8.** Killed by Zenith in transformed state, episode 178. The transformation cost is the highest yet — Zenith forgets a year.

**Function:** The dark mirror of the "save them by waking them" temptation.

---

## IRON LAWS OF THE FORMAT

1. **One job per episode.** Reveal, escalate, choice, shift, or hook. Never two.
2. **The last 2 seconds matter most.** They determine whether the viewer comes back.
3. **No catch-up exposition.** Trust the audience. Reward rewatchers.
4. **Every transformation is a funeral.** Show the cost.
5. **Title and end card never change format.** The container is the brand.

---

## WHAT THIS SERIES IS NOT

To stay disciplined, what Zenith should never become:

- A superhero show
- A "find the artifacts" quest narrative
- A romance plot with a sci-fi coat of paint
- A philosophy lecture
- An ensemble show — Zenith is always the center, always
- A series with a sequel hook — this story ends

---

## THE NORTH STAR

When in doubt about any creative decision, return to this:

> *Zenith is the last of his people. He came to Earth looking for kin. He found something better, and worse: he found that his people once chose to be smaller, and that this was the greatest thing they ever did. The series is the story of him learning to make the same choice. Everything else is decoration.*
