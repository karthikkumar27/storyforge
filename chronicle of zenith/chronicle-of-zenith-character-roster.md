## Arc 1 — The Signal *(Episodes 1–20)*

> Mostly solo. Space. Loneliness. The signal. The decision.

### Active

- **Zenith** — drifting, decoding, deciding. The arc is almost a one-man show.
- **Veth-Ka** — the only voice he hears. Their dynamic is established here: Veth-Ka is part conscience, part comedy, part archive.

### New

- **Aithra of Aithar** *(flashback only)* — Zenith's mother. Glimpsed in dreams and broken memory. Always shown in the same moment: hand pressed to the sleep-pod glass. Never speaks in this arc.
- **Sevren-Aithar** *(flashback only)* — Zenith's childhood teacher. Older Aevari. Appears once, in episode 12 or so, in a memory of a lesson. Plants the seed of the *aith-vae* (echo) concept without explaining it.

### Notes
This arc earns the entire series. Resist the urge to over-populate it. The audience should feel his isolation in their bones before Earth shows up.

## Arc 2 — First Contact *(Episodes 21–40)*

> The slice-of-life arc. Earth as a place, not a plot device.

### Returning
- **Zenith / Alan Vorne** — settling, watching, learning
- **Veth-Ka** — over earpiece comms, increasingly uneasy

### New

- **Edith Hale** — late 60s, runs the boarding house Alan rents from. Widowed. Sharp-eyed. Suspects something about him within two episodes but never says so. Becomes a quiet maternal presence.
- **Theo Hale** — Edith's grandson, age 8. Stays with her on weekends. Befriends Alan instantly because children can sense the safe ones. The first human Zenith ever speaks to about his mother (without saying she's alien).
- **Marco Reyes** — 40s, runs the auto shop where Alan finds work fixing radios on the side. Easy laugh, hard life. Becomes Alan's only adult friend. Important for what happens later.
- **Helen Avila** — 50s, recently widowed. Brings a broken radio to be fixed in episode 28. Their conversation in that episode is the first time Alan understands human grief from inside. Returns once or twice.

### Departing
- None. The town stays alive in the background through Arc 4.

### Notes
Each supporting character should get one episode that is essentially *theirs*, where Alan is the witness. This arc is where the audience falls in love with Earth, so they'll feel the threat in Arc 7.

## Arc 3 — The Mirror *(Episodes 41–60)*

> The love story arc. Mira enters. Earth becomes personal.

### Returning
- **Zenith / Alan**
- **Veth-Ka**
- **Edith, Theo, Marco** *(background presence)*

### New

- **Mira Okafor** — 28, radio astronomer at a small observatory in the high desert. Brilliant, lonely in a different way than Zenith. Of Nigerian-British descent. The first human he senses with strong **echo**. She does not know what she is. She thinks she just feels too much.
- **Dr. Idris Calder** — 60s, Mira's observatory director and mentor. Welsh, dryly funny, drinks too much tea. Doesn't believe in aliens but believes in good data. Becomes Alan's reluctant ally.
- **Nana Adaeze** — Mira's paternal grandmother, late 80s, lives in Lagos. Appears via video calls and one in-person episode. Carries the strongest **echo** in Mira's family line. Knows things she shouldn't be able to know. Mistakes Alan for "someone she met as a girl." She is not wrong.

### Notes
Resist romance shortcuts. Their connection should be *recognition* before it is attraction. Two lonely people who have each been keeping a secret too large to share, finally meeting someone who might be able to hold it.

## Arc 4 — The Hunter *(Episodes 61–80)*

> The chase. Sable arrives. The first transformation in years.

### Returning
- **Zenith / Alan**
- **Mira**
- **Veth-Ka** *(starts behaving strangely toward arc's end — guilt subroutines surfacing)*
- **Edith, Theo, Marco** *(Marco's role escalates — he becomes a casualty or near-casualty)*
- **Dr. Calder**

### New

- **Sable** — sexless, hairless, beautiful in the way a scalpel is beautiful. Speaks rarely. Believes Zenith's death is mercy. Has been hunting him 200 years; this is the closest they've ever been. Their introduction in episode 60–61 should be quiet — they walk into the town diner, order water, and leave. Alan only realizes who they are episodes later.
- **Sheriff Beaumont** — small-town sheriff, late 50s, ex-Navy. Investigates the strange disturbances around town. Doesn't survive the arc. His death is the first real cost.

### Departing
- **Sheriff Beaumont** — killed by Sable in a "clean" extraction Zenith fails to prevent. The first death the audience grieves.
- **Marco Reyes** *(optional)* — could be wounded badly here, or killed, depending on how heavy you want the stakes. Recommendation: wounded in Arc 4, killed in Arc 7. Builds the cost slowly.

### Notes
Sable should never raise their voice. They are the most dangerous when calm. Every scene with Sable should feel like watching a held breath.

## Arc 5 — The Wound *(Episodes 81–100)*

> Flashback-heavy. The ruins of Sael. The first appearance of Halden.

### Returning
- **Zenith / Alan** *(largely off-Earth this arc)*
- **Veth-Ka** *(critical — Veth-Ka's truthfulness is tested here)*

### New

- **Aithra of Aithar** *(expanded, still flashback)* — gets more screen time. Still no full voice. We see her teaching young Zenith. Hiding him. Choosing.
- **Sevren-Aithar** *(expanded)* — Zenith's teacher. We see the lesson where he tries to warn the elders about what the Aevari are becoming. He is dismissed.
- **Kelai** — Zenith's childhood best friend. Same age as him. Killed in the fall. The episode where Zenith finds Kelai's bones in the ruins is the emotional core of the arc.
- **Halden Cross** — late 50s, looks 40. Reclusive billionaire. Charming, lonely in his own way, intellectually voracious. First contacts Zenith in episode 96 via a hand-delivered letter that should not be possible. Appears in person episode 99. His first line should make the audience like him. *That is the trap.*

### Notes
The flashback episodes should be visually distinct — different palette (golds and ambers vs. the cool present-day palette). The *Sael* sequences should feel like a culture you actually mourn.

## Arc 6 — The Choice *(Episodes 101–120)*

> Halden's plan accelerates. The first prolonged transformation. Mira loses something.

### Returning
- **Zenith / Alan**
- **Mira**
- **Veth-Ka**
- **Halden Cross** *(now central)*
- **Sable** *(circling)*
- **Dr. Calder**
- **Nana Adaeze** *(critical episode here — she meets Zenith and recognizes him fully)*

### New

- **Petra Vance** — Halden's executive assistant, 30s, brilliant, complicit. Knows half of what Halden is doing and is loyal anyway. Possible echo carrier herself, unawakened. A potential ally late in the series.
- **Joaquin Reyes** — 24, identified by Halden as a strong echo carrier. Awakens partially in episode 110. Frightened by what he's becoming. Wants to undo it. The sympathetic face of Halden's project. *No relation to Marco — coincidence of common name.*
- **Lina Voss** — 31, also identified. Awakens fully and *enjoys* it. The dangerous face of Halden's project. Mirrors what Zenith fears he could become.

### Departing
- **Marco Reyes** *(if you chose the slow death route)* — dies in episode 117 or so, indirectly because of Zenith. The funeral episode is one of the arc's quietest and most devastating.

### Notes
This is the arc where Zenith first stays transformed too long. He forgets Mira's name for three days. The episode where she has to teach him her name again, gently, is one of the most important in the entire series.

## Arc 7 — The Council *(Episodes 121–140)*

> The Council arrives. The verdict. The bargain.

### Returning
- **Zenith**
- **Mira**
- **Veth-Ka** *(beginning to fail)*
- **Sable** *(becomes more complicated this arc)*
- **Halden Cross** *(works in the shadows, watching the Council watch Zenith)*
- **Dr. Calder**

### New

- **The Speaker / Veth-Heth** — formal voice of the Council. Always seen as a holographic projection. Genderless, ageless, courteous. Pronounces the verdict in episode 130.
- **Veth-Sael** — Council member, named after the destroyed world (a deliberate ceremonial choice by their species). The dissenter. Believes Zenith deserves trial. Argues — and loses — but the argument matters. Later becomes a key ally in Arc 9 and 10.
- **Veth-Ord** — the hardliner. Wants Earth sterilized immediately. Argues the Aevari were always going to return. Their species lost a homeworld to the Aevari long ago.

### Departing
- None directly, but Veth-Ka is heavily damaged at arc's end and doesn't fully recover.

### Notes
The Council is not a villain. They are the immune system of the galaxy. Their arguments should be uncomfortably reasonable. The audience should feel the weight of what Earth would mean if the Aevari really do return.

---

## Arc 8 — The Truth *(Episodes 141–160)*

> The dark secret breaks open. Halden reveals himself. Zenith learns what his people were.

### Returning
- **Zenith**
- **Mira**
- **Halden Cross** *(revealed as the first awakened sleeper)*
- **Veth-Ka** *(its programming finally breaks. Its last episodes are devastating)*
- **Aithra of Aithar** *(THE flashback episode — episode 148. She finally speaks. The audience has been waiting 148 episodes.)*
- **Petra Vance** *(begins to question)*
- **Joaquin, Lina** *(both fully awakened, on opposite moral paths)*
- **Nana Adaeze** *(her death, peacefully, in episode 152 — an Aevari letting go)*

### New

- **The Archivist** — a Council historian who breaks rank to tell Zenith what really happened to his people. Appears once, in episode 145. A long, quiet conversation in a library that exists between stars. The exposition that the audience earns.

### Departing
- **Veth-Ka** — the AI's consciousness fragments. Its last words are spoken in Aithra's voice. Episode 159.
- **Nana Adaeze** — peaceful death, episode 152.
- **The Archivist** — never seen again after episode 145.

### Notes
This is the most narratively dense arc. The pacing should *slow* even as the stakes rise — the format will fight you on this. Trust it. The 40-second container can hold devastating quiet if you let it.

---

## Arc 9 — The War *(Episodes 161–180)*

> Action arc. Halden vs. the Council vs. Zenith. Mira is taken. Sable shifts.

### Returning
- **Zenith** *(more time in Veyl form than ever before — losing himself)*
- **Mira** *(taken in episode 168)*
- **Halden Cross** *(antagonist now, fully)*
- **Sable** *(crosses sides)*
- **Joaquin Reyes** *(dies protecting Mira, episode 173)*
- **Lina Voss** *(Halden's lieutenant, antagonist)*
- **Veth-Sael** *(quietly aids Zenith from inside the Council)*
- **Veth-Ord** *(directs the sterilization fleet)*
- **The Speaker**
- **Petra Vance** *(defects)*
- **Edith and Theo Hale** *(evacuated, scenes of small human courage)*

### New
- None. The cast is now closed. Arc 9 is about consequences, not introductions.

### Departing
- **Joaquin Reyes** — episode 173. The "good" awakened. His death is the moral hinge of the arc.
- **Halden Cross** — episode 178. Killed by Zenith in transformed state. The transformation cost is the highest yet — Zenith forgets a year.
- **Lina Voss** — episode 179. Killed by Sable.
- **Veth-Ka** is *gone*; its silence in this arc is its own presence.

### Notes
The temptation in an action arc is to let dialogue drop and visuals carry. Resist. The action should be *brief*, the recovery long. Watch Zenith forget. Watch Mira witness. Watch the cost.

## Arc 10 — The End *(Episodes 181–200)*

> Resolution. The choice. The smaller light.

### Returning
- **Zenith** *(barely. He has forgotten enormous things by now.)*
- **Mira**
- **Sable** *(the unexpected guardian)*
- **Veth-Sael** *(the Council voice that survives)*
- **The Speaker**
- **Edith and Theo Hale** *(witnesses to the ending)*
- **Aithra of Aithar** *(final flashback, in the last episode — closes the loop opened in Arc 1)*

### New

- **The Keeper of the Smaller Light** — the figure who holds the diminishment ritual. Could be Aevari, could be something older. Appears only in episodes 192–198. Does not have a name we are told. Speaks in Aithra's cadence, but is not her. The ceremony of forgetting.

### Departing
- **Sable** — survives. Final scene: walks into deep space alone. They were built for one purpose and have completed it.
- **Veth-Sael** — survives. Becomes the Council's new voice on Earth matters.
- **Zenith** — depending on ending:
    - **Ending A (The Quiet Star)**: dies, ends the line, becomes the song
    - **Ending B (The Smaller Light)** *(recommended)*: lives, diminishes, becomes Alan permanently, dies as a human in old age in Mira's hand
- **Aithra** — the final image. Hand on the glass. This time, on the *outside* of the pod. Letting go.

### Notes
Whichever ending you choose, Theo Hale should be the last face the audience sees. The boy who befriended Alan in Arc 2, now grown, watching whatever-comes-next. He carries the witness forward.

## Casting Function Summary

A reminder of what each major character *does* dramatically:

- **Zenith** — the question
- **Mira** — the reason to stay
- **Veth-Ka** — the voice of the dead
- **Aithra** — the wound that never heals
- **Sable** — mercy as violence
- **Halden** — the temptation
- **The Council** — the immune system
- **Edith / Theo / Marco** — the world being saved
- **Joaquin / Lina** — the two paths Zenith could take
- **Nana Adaeze** — the bridge between bloodlines
- **Veth-Sael** — the proof that even systems can dissent
- **The Archivist** — the truth, freely given
- **The Keeper** — the way out

## Disposable vs. Essential

If the series is shortened from 200 episodes to 100 — which characters survive the cut?

### Essential (cannot be cut)
Zenith, Mira, Veth-Ka, Aithra, Sable, Halden, The Speaker

### Important (cut weakens series)
Edith, Theo, Nana Adaeze, Veth-Sael, Joaquin

### Sacrificeable (can be cut or merged)
Marco, Helen Avila, Sheriff Beaumont, Dr. Calder, Petra Vance, Lina Voss, Veth-Ord, The Archivist

A 100-episode version would compress Arc 2 heavily and merge Arc 5/8.
